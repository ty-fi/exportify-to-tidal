Move a Spotify playlist to Tidal

Someone sent you a Spotify playlist and you use Tidal. This rebuilds it in your Tidal account, matching each track by its recording ID so you get the same recording rather than a cover or a live version.

Everything runs in this browser. Your playlist file is never uploaded anywhere, and your Tidal login goes directly to Tidal — this page never sees your password and never stores your account details on a server.

1

Get the playlist out of Spotify

Spotify doesn't let tools like this one read a playlist that belongs to someone else, so you have to export it yourself first. It takes about a minute and you only need a free Spotify account.

  1. Follow the playlist in Spotify. Open it and press Follow (the heart or Follow button) so it appears in your own library. Exportify can only see playlists that are in your library.
  2. Open Exportify and sign in with Spotify. It only asks for read access to your playlists.
  3. Find the playlist in the list and press Export. Your browser downloads a .csv file.
  4. You can unfollow the playlist in Spotify afterwards if you want.
Open Exportify ↗
Make sure it's exportify.app — there is an unrelated site called exportify.net. They look similar, but only exportify.app includes the recording IDs this page matches on. If you used the wrong one, this page will tell you when you drop the file.
2

Connect your Tidal account

You'll be sent to Tidal to sign in, then returned here. This page only asks for permission to read and create playlists.

3

Drop the file you downloaded

Drag the .csv here, or click to browse. If you used Exportify's Export All button you'll have a .zip — drop that instead and pick which playlist you want.

Why some tracks don't match. Matching is by ISRC, the recording ID that identifies a specific studio take. When Tidal's catalogue has a different ID for the same recording, this falls back to comparing title, artist, album and length — and refuses anything whose length is too far off, because that usually means a cover, a remix or a live version. Unmatched tracks are listed so you can add them by hand.
